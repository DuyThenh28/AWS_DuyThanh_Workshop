## Participation Date

**Date:** July 25, 2026

---

# Purpose of the Event

The event marked the conclusion of **AWS AI Build Week**, where teams presented their AI projects, shared development experiences, and learned from real-world solutions built on AWS.

---

# Main Content

## 1. AI Project Demonstrations

Teams showcased the AI applications they had developed during AI Build Week.

Featured projects included:

- Hackathon Journey
- SignalScout
- Solution Architect Professional Native App

These presentations provided valuable insights into applying AI and AWS services to real-world business problems.

---

## 2. Solution Architecture Sharing

Each team explained its system architecture, development workflow, and AWS services used throughout the project.

They also shared implementation challenges and optimization strategies.

---

## 3. Networking with Mentors and Teams

Participants had the opportunity to interact with AWS mentors and development teams, discussing technical solutions, project ideas, and future improvements.

---

## 4. Program Closing

The Community Day concluded the AI Build Week program by highlighting practical experiences in AI development, teamwork, and cloud solution implementation on AWS.
