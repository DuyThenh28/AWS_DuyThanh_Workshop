| **Information** | **Details** |
|:----------------|:------------|

| **📍 Location** | 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City |
| **👤 Role** | Participant |

### 📝 Description

The workshop introduced the application of **Generative AI** in application and database modernization on the AWS platform. The program included technical presentations from AWS experts, live demonstrations of cloud solutions, and interactive discussions with participants.

### 🎯 Outcomes and Achievements

- Gained a better understanding of Generative AI and its applications in software development.
- Expanded my knowledge of AWS Cloud services and database modernization.
- Learned practical experience and best practices shared by AWS experts.
- Improved communication, listening, and problem-solving skills.
- Applied the knowledge gained to the development of the **Saxophone Store Website** project.
